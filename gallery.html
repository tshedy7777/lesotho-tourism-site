Scenic Lesotho Gallery

Explore the unmatched beauty of the Kingdom in the Sky through our interactive sliding grid.

Maletsunyane Falls

A magnificent 192-meter plume of water plummeting over majestic basalt cliffs into a dramatic cloud-rimmed gorge near Semonkong.

Katse Dam Reservoir

An incredible engineering marvel tucked inside the high mountains, producing smooth channels of bright blue highland waters.

Afriski Slopes

Southern Africa's premier high-altitude ski resort, offering fresh winter snowboarding tracks and rugged summer adventure trails.

Sani Pass Switchbacks

The legendary 4x4 mountain pass weaving through steep valley walls right up to the highest pub in Africa.

Ts'ehlanyane National Park

Vast mountain valleys holding rare high-altitude forests, refreshing mountain streams, and rugged horse-riding wilderness trails.

Sehlabathebe National Park

An ancient alpine plateau rich with unique sandstone formations, pristine rock pools, wild orchids, and wild horses roaming free.

Bokong Nature Reserve

High rocky peaks overlooking dramatic valleys, home to the famous Lepaqoa waterfall which freezes into a huge sheet of solid ice during mid-winter.

Thaba-Bosiu Fortress Mountain

The sacred flat-topped mountain fortress where King Moshoeshoe I united clans and founded the historical birthplace of the Basotho nation.

Ha-Kome Cave Houses

Remarkable mud and thatch dwellings built completely inside hidden cave alcoves over 200 years ago, still occupied by original clan families.

Liphofung Cave Sanctuary

A deep historic sandstone overhang sheltering colorful, priceless ancient San rock paintings and early archaeological stone tools near Oxbow.
