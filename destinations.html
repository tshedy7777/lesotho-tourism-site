Explore Lesotho

Here are the best tourist destinations in Lesotho

Waterfalls & Adventure

Maletsunyane Falls - Semonkong

One of the highest single-drop waterfalls in Africa at 192m. You can do the world's highest commercial abseil here, plus pony trekking.

Katse Dam

Second largest dam in Africa, part of the Highlands Water Project.

Afriski & Mahlasela - Butha-Buthe

Only ski resort in Lesotho, open for skiing in winter (June-Aug), and for mountain biking/adventure in summer.

Mountains & National Parks

Sani Pass

The famous pass from South Africa to Lesotho. You need a 4x4. On top is the Sani Mountain Lodge with the highest pub in Africa.

Ts'ehlanyane National Park

Best for hiking, forests, waterfalls, Basotho pony trails. Home to Maliba Lodge.

Sehlabathebe National Park

UNESCO World Heritage Site with the Drakensberg. Rock art, rock formations, wild horses, very remote and wild.

Bokong Nature Reserve

Near Katse, has the famous Lepaqoa Waterfall that freezes in winter.

History And Culture

Thaba-Bosiu

The mountain fortress where King Moshoeshoe The First founded the Basotho nation. Museum and cultural village at the foot.

Ha-Kome Cave Houses

200-year-old homes built inside caves, still inhabited by descendants of the original clan.

Liphofung Cave - Near Oxbow

San rock art painting inside a large sandstone rock.

Morija

Historic mission town with Morija Museum & Archives, Lesotho's oldest museum and home to the Morija Arts Festival.

Cities & Towns

Maseru

Capital, for markets, Basotho Hat craft market, and nightlife.

Malealea

Small village run by community tourism. Best for authentic village homestays, horse trails, and pony trekking school visits.

Mokhotlong & Leribe

For diamond mines views and weaving cooperatives.
