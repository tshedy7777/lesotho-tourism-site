Activities to do in Lesotho

Abseiling at Maletsunyane Falls

Descend the 204-meter single-drop waterfall in Semonkong, holder of a Guinness World Record for the commercial single-drop abseil.

Pony Trekking

Ride traditional Basotho ponies through mountain trails, deep gorges, and high altitude passes, with popular departures from Semonkong Lodge and Malealea Lodge.

Hiking & Camping

Explore rugged terrain in the Maluti Mountains, Ts'ehlanyane National Park and Sehlabathebe National Park.

Fly Fishing

Cast for brown and rainbow trout in the rivers and streams around Semonkong and the Lesotho highlands.

Quad Biking

Thrill through the dirt tracks and mountain paths on rugged multi-terrain quad bikes across remote Basotho mountain villages.

Skiing

Experience crisp winter sports high up on the slopes of the Maluti Mountains at Africa's pristine high-altitude winter snow resorts.
