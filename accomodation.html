In Lesotho, most tourists stay in Maseru for comfort, and in the mountain lodges outside the city for adventure. These are the places that are best set up to accomodate visitors.

In the low lands

The options best for first night, near border, banks and malls.

\
  1. Avani Lesotho/Maseru Hotel & Casino

    2. Full service,pool,restaurant, conference facilities and main luxury option in the capital.

  2. Mpilo Boutique Hotel, The Palace, Blue Mountain INN

    3. Mid-range options in Maseru, quieter/safer than the city centre.

  3. Maliba lodge 7 Maliba Mountain Chalet

    4. 5-star, family friendly, hiking skiing, horse ridding,archery, kids club. The most rated lodge in the country right now.

  4. Malealea & Semonkong

    5. Semonkong Lodge- riverside lodge with mountain views, base for Maletsunyane Falls abseil and pony treks.

    Malealea Lodge- famous community lodge with rooms, rondavels and camping, hub for pony trekking and hiking.

Highlands

  1. Katse Lodge

    2. Overlooking Katse Dam main confortable stay for exploring central highlands, boating and trout fishing.

  2. Mohale Lodge

    3. Right at Mohale Dam

  3. Sani Mountain Lodge

    4. At the top of Sani Pass in Mokhotlong, has the highest pub in all of Africa.

Cultural & Historical

  1. Thaba Bosiu Cultural Village

    2. Traditional accomodation with cultural tours near the historic mountain fortress.

  2. Roma

    3. University town 35km from Maseru, calm and cheap lodging in a green valley, good alternative to Maseru.
